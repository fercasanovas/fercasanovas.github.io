What Is an AI Visibility Audit?

By CassaInnova · Updated 20 September 2026

An AI visibility audit checks whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google's AI Overviews name a brand for the buying questions in its category. It asks each question more than once, on a fixed list, and scores the results. The output is a documented, comparable number — not a single chat session, a guess, or a promise about future rankings.

What Does an AI Visibility Audit Actually Measure?

An AI visibility audit asks one narrow question: does an answer engine's generated response name a specific brand, and how. It runs on a fixed list of buyer-shaped questions, asked the way a real prospect would type them — not keyword strings.

  • Platform and record. Each question goes to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google's AI Overviews; the consultant notes whether the brand is named, where, how accurately, and alongside which competitors.
  • Repeated over time. Each question runs more than once, on different days — a brand present Monday and absent Wednesday hasn't lost anything, it's revealed the answer is unstable.
  • Scale. About twelve buyer questions, four intent types, four platforms — roughly 48 individually tracked results.
  • Site check. Roughly twenty-five checks across five areas, feeding a 0–100 composite from four named sub-scores.

The four intent types

  • Browsing. No vendor named yet — what are the options.
  • Comparing. Two named brands, side by side.
  • Ready to hire. A real constraint — budget, timeline, location.
  • Displacing. A competitor already named; what else exists.

What an AI Visibility Audit Is Not

The category gets confused with three adjacent things.

  • Not a single ChatGPT session. One signed-in query, asked once, shows what that account saw — not what a stranger asking the same question tomorrow would see.
  • Not a rank-tracking dashboard. A subscription tool reporting a number moving weekly is reporting a metric, not an audit. It usually can't say why the number moved, on which platform, or which page caused it.
  • Not a guarantee. No credible version promises a brand will be cited or will rank. Engines select sources by criteria they don't publish and change without notice — nobody, including the platforms, can promise a specific outcome.

How Is an AI Visibility Audit Different From a Regular SEO Audit?

A technical SEO audit and an AI visibility audit overlap at the edges. They answer different questions.

Traditional SEO auditAI visibility audit
Core questionDoes this page rank in search results?Does an answer engine name this brand when asked?
Unit measuredRankings, backlinks, page speed, crawl errorsCitation, characterization, and source footprint across four answer engines
OutputA list of on-page and technical fixesA scored result plus a prioritized fix list, tied to logged evidence
Where they overlapCrawlability and indexability matter to bothA complete audit checks whether AI-specific crawlers can read a site — a separate question from whether Googlebot can

The overlap is real: a site an AI crawler cannot fetch is invisible to both problems, for the same underlying reason. But a technically flawless SEO site can still be poorly described, or entirely absent, in an AI-generated answer. Clean crawlability is necessary — it is nowhere near sufficient.

What Does a Consultant Deliver at the End of an AI Visibility Audit?

A finished audit hands over three things a client can act on immediately.

  • A scored result, tied to its date range. Answers move over time; an honest report names the window it was measured in, rather than presenting the number as a permanent fact.
  • A prioritized fix list. Ordered by what blocks a citation, not what merely helps — two consultants working from the same findings should agree on it.
  • An evidence trail. The logged answers behind every finding, not a summary — verifiable by hand, not taken on faith.

Who Actually Runs an AI Visibility Audit?

Independent SEO consultants and small agencies run these audits today. In-house marketing teams don't. Neither do the large AEO-specialist shops charging five figures a month.

  • Too specific and too manual to be a $50-a-month dashboard.
  • Too small an engagement to interest an agency built around retainers.
  • The most common adopter already sells a fixed-scope technical audit and is adding this next — usually because a client asked first, unprompted: "are we even showing up in ChatGPT?"

No specific citation, ranking, or traffic outcome is guaranteed for any brand; answer engines select their sources using undisclosed criteria that change without notice.
