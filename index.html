What Is an AI Visibility Audit?

By CassaInnova · Updated 20 September 2026

An AI visibility audit checks whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google's AI Overviews name a brand for the buying questions in its category. It asks each question more than once, on a fixed list, and scores the results. The output is a documented, comparable number — not a single chat session, a guess, or a promise about future rankings.

What Does an AI Visibility Audit Actually Measure?

An AI visibility audit is narrower than a search ranking. It asks whether an answer engine's generated response names a specific brand at all — and if so, how.

A consultant runs a fixed list of buyer-shaped questions. These are the kind a real prospect would type or ask — not keyword strings. Each question goes to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google's AI Overviews. The consultant records what comes back: is the brand named, where in the answer, described accurately, and alongside which competitors.

Answers aren't fixed. The same question gets asked more than once, on different days, before any single outcome is trusted. A brand that appears Monday and is absent Wednesday hasn't lost anything between the two checks. It has revealed that the true answer is unstable. An audit that asks only once reports a coin flip as a fact.

A complete audit typically runs about twelve buyer questions across four intent types. One type is a buyer just browsing the category. Another is a buyer comparing two named brands. A third is a buyer ready to hire under a real constraint. The fourth is a buyer who already named a competitor and is asking what else exists. Checked on all four platforms, that's roughly 48 individually tracked results — not one impression from one chat window.

Alongside the engine-facing measurement, a complete audit also reviews the client's own site. That typically covers around twenty-five checks across five areas — from whether AI-specific crawlers can read the pages, to whether the brand's name and category description stay consistent online. The headline number is usually a 0–100 composite built from four named sub-scores, not one opaque figure.

What an AI Visibility Audit Is Not

The category attracts confusion, mostly by getting lumped in with three adjacent things.

It is not a single ChatGPT session. Asking "how do we show up in ChatGPT" once, from a signed-in account with your own history behind it, tells you what that account saw once. It doesn't tell you what a stranger asking the same question tomorrow would see.

It is not a rank-tracking dashboard. A subscription tool reporting a number moving up or down each week is reporting a metric, not an audit. It usually can't say why the number moved, on which platform, or what page caused it.

It is not a guarantee. No credible version of this service promises a brand will be cited, will rank, or will appear in a given answer. The engines select sources using criteria they don't publish, and they change those criteria without notice. Anyone promising a specific outcome is promising something nobody can deliver — not even the platforms themselves.

How Is an AI Visibility Audit Different From a Regular SEO Audit?

A technical SEO audit and an AI visibility audit overlap at the edges. They answer different questions.

| | Traditional SEO audit | AI visibility audit | | --- | --- | --- | | Core question | Does this page rank in search results? | Does an answer engine name this brand when asked? | | Unit measured | Rankings, backlinks, page speed, crawl errors | Citation, characterization, and source footprint across four answer engines | | Output | A list of on-page and technical fixes | A scored result plus a prioritized fix list, tied to logged evidence | | Where they overlap | Crawlability and indexability matter to both | A complete audit checks whether AI-specific crawlers can read a site — a separate question from whether Googlebot can |

The overlap is real and worth naming plainly: a site an AI crawler cannot fetch is invisible to both problems, for the same underlying reason. But a technically flawless SEO site can still be poorly described, or entirely absent, in an AI-generated answer. Clean crawlability is necessary. It is nowhere near sufficient.

What Does a Consultant Deliver at the End of an AI Visibility Audit?

A finished AI visibility audit produces three things a client can act on immediately.

A scored result, tied to a specific date range. Because the underlying answers move over time, an honest report states the window it was measured in — rather than presenting a number as a permanent fact.

A prioritized fix list, ordered by what actually blocks a citation versus what merely helps at the margin. A crawler blocked outright from reading the site outranks a missing directory listing. The ordering should be one two different consultants, working from the same findings, would independently agree on.

An evidence trail — the actual logged answers behind every finding, not a summary of them. A skeptical client, or a skeptical consultant checking a colleague's work, can verify any single claim by hand.

Who Actually Runs an AI Visibility Audit?

Independent SEO consultants and small agencies are the ones running these audits today. In-house marketing teams don't run them. Neither do the large AEO-specialist shops charging five figures a month.

The work sits between the two. It's too specific and too manual to be a $50-a-month dashboard, and too small an engagement to interest an agency built around retainers.

A consultant who already sells a fixed-scope technical audit is typically the one adding this next. The client is usually the one who asked for it first — on a call, unprompted, with a question that sounds like "are we even showing up in ChatGPT?"

No specific citation, ranking, or traffic outcome is guaranteed for any brand; answer engines select their sources using undisclosed criteria that change without notice.
