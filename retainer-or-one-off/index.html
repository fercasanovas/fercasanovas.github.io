Should You Sell an AI Visibility Audit as a Retainer or a One-Off?

By CassaInnova · Updated 20 September 2026

Sell it as a one-off, re-quoted at ninety days, not a monthly retainer. The arithmetic doesn't support a retainer: this is a quarterly instrument, and a monthly fee prices for work that isn't being delivered between measurements. A retainer works only once real monthly deliverables (content, implementation, monitoring) are added on top.

What Does the Arithmetic Actually Say?

Look at four quarterly measurements a year, annualized. Compare that against what the market charges for an ongoing program.

  • Even at the top of the band. A retainer built on measurement alone lands at the very bottom of the market. That's still below what a real monthly program charges.
  • The gap has to be filled with something else. Real monthly work, not more frequent measurement.

Why Doesn't Measuring More Often Fix the Math?

  • Measuring monthly doesn't produce monthly news. The roadmap a fix list is built to fit runs on a quarterly horizon. Checking before the work has shipped just re-measures the same unfinished quarter.
  • More frequent runs add noise, not signal. A result checked weekly moves for reasons that have nothing to do with the client's own site.
  • The market already has a name for a quarterly instrument billed monthly. It's the complaint buyers in this category raise most often.

What Should You Actually Sell Instead?

  • Quote the baseline audit, then quote the follow-up separately. Price it at roughly the same fee, scheduled when it's due. Don't fold it into a monthly number.
  • Schedule the re-audit at the walkthrough, while the fix list is still in front of the client. It's the best-paying work you'll do. The second engagement costs you almost nothing extra to win.
  • If you do sell a retainer, treat the measurement as one line item inside it, not the whole product. Price the rest, content, implementation, monitoring, separately. This instrument doesn't ship a method for any of it.

What Would Actually Justify a Monthly Number?

  • Real delivery between measurements. Writing or rewriting the pages a fix list names. Earning the coverage a footprint gap calls for, or building out missing structured data.
  • Not more measurement. Checking again sooner than the roadmap ships work produces noise, not a reason to bill monthly.

Deciding this is the first half of how to add a new service to an agency's pricing. The model question comes before the number does. Whichever model you land on, it still has to sit inside what an AI visibility audit should cost rather than compete with it.
