What Should an AI Visibility Audit Cost?

By CassaInnova · Updated 20 September 2026

An AI visibility audit runs $1,500 to $3,000 as a one-time engagement, covering one brand, one domain and one competitive set across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews. Below that range, a tool is standing in for a person; above it, most of the fee is agency overhead on the same diagnosis.

What Determines Where in the $1,500–$3,000 Range a Quote Lands?

Two things move a quote inside the range — not how well a consultant negotiates.

The two factors

  • Scope size. A smaller, single-competitive-set list sits near the bottom of the range; a bigger battery — more questions, deeper competitor coverage — sits near the top, because the range tracks hours actually spent, not negotiation.
  • Existing information. A client with an already-documented category, competitor list, and priority pages costs less setup time than a brand-new prospect — and that difference should show in the quote, not the fee.

Why Doesn't an AI Visibility Audit Cost $250 Like the Automated Tools?

An automated scan and a consultant-run audit aren't the same product, even on the same comparison page.

  • The scan. $250–$500 for a single snapshot from a single account — a number with nothing behind it a client can check.
  • The audit. A logged evidence trail: the actual questions, dates, and answers, saved and handed over.
  • The test. A skeptical client — or a skeptical consultant reviewing someone else's work — can verify any single finding by hand, instead of trusting a dashboard's math.

Why Doesn't an AI Visibility Audit Cost $8,000–$15,000 Like an AEO Agency?

An agency retainer in that range bundles the same diagnosis with strategy, implementation, and monitoring — a program, not a single measurement. A one-time audit at $1,500–$3,000 stops at the diagnosis: a scored result, a prioritized fix list, and the evidence behind both. It skips the agency overhead and the fix-list execution — real services, just a different engagement.

What Does the Fee for an AI Visibility Audit Actually Cover?

The fee covers hours — and they don't compress much no matter who's doing the work.

  • Every question, twice. Each runs at least a day apart, across four platforms — a single answer from a changing system is a data point, not a finding.
  • The client's own site. Whether relevant crawlers can read it, whether the brand's described consistently online, and whether there's a factual footprint an engine could draw from.
  • A written, ordered result. What to fix first — not a raw data dump.

None of that compresses to an hour — that's why the floor of this range sits where it does.

Is a Monthly Retainer the Right Model for This Work?

Usually not — the arithmetic decides it, not a stylistic preference.

Retainer vs. single audit

RetainerSingle audit, priced right
CadenceMonthly billingRoughly every 90 days — enough time for a fix list to ship and a re-measurement to mean something
Typical price$1,000–$2,500/month for an entry-level search programA few hundred dollars a month once a single audit fee is annualized
What it buysWork between measurementsThe measurement itself

The more defensible model: quote a baseline audit, then quote the follow-up separately, at roughly the same fee, when it's due. That's different from packaging a quarterly instrument as if it were a monthly service.
