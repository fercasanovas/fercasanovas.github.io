How Do You Scope an AI Visibility Engagement?

By CassaInnova · Updated 20 September 2026

Scoping an AI visibility engagement means fixing four facts before you quote: the domain, the category phrase, the competitive set, and which of two battery sizes you'll run. Lock those four and the price follows almost mechanically. Skip one and the quote becomes a guess with a number attached.

What Four Facts Do You Need Before You Can Quote?

Get these in the first call, before anything becomes a written proposal. A quote built without all four gets redone once the real answers come in.

The four facts

  • Domain. One domain, one brand — a second domain or a second brand line is a separate engagement, not a bigger version of this one.
  • Category phrase. The specific words a buyer would use to describe what the client sells, not the client's internal name for it.
  • Competitive set. Who actually shows up when a buyer asks the category question — not the client's self-selected list of rivals.
  • Battery size. Core, or Core + Extended. This is the one lever that actually changes scope.

Core or Core + Extended: How Do You Pick?

Pick the tier from the decision in front of the client, never from what they can afford. That single rule keeps the conversation about scope instead of budget.

  • Core is the default, full stop. It's the first engagement for every client, regardless of size.
  • Core + Extended earns its place, not its budget — a client needing per-angle findings instead of one composite is a real reason; a bigger invoice is not.

What Pushes a Scope Into a Different, Larger Engagement?

Three things move an engagement outside a normal single quote. Each is a structural mismatch, not a negotiation.

  • More than one domain, or more than one distinct category phrase. The instrument is built from one of each — two of either is two engagements.
  • A jurisdiction set the standard battery can't carry. A client licensed across several states needs location-qualified prompts per state, and each added jurisdiction adds its own battery.
  • A turnaround that won't fit the run window. Every cell is checked twice, a day or more apart, inside one measurement window — rushing that is a calendar problem, not a fee problem.

How Long Does a Properly Scoped Engagement Actually Take?

  • Steady-state Core: roughly eleven hours, once the method is familiar.
  • Steady-state Core + Extended: seventeen to twenty hours.
  • A genuine first-ever audit — a consultant's own first run of the method — commonly runs thirteen to seventeen hours. The method is being learned alongside the work.
  • None of it compresses into a weekend. Every cell is checked at least twice inside a seven-day run window, sitting inside a roughly three-week engagement calendar.

What Should the Scoping Call Actually Cover?

The call exists to fill in the four facts above with the client's real answers, not a consultant's assumptions.

  • Category phrase and competitors, in the client's own words.
  • Priority pages — the handful the technical review will actually check.
  • Jurisdiction and timeline, so a scope trigger gets caught before the quote, not after.
