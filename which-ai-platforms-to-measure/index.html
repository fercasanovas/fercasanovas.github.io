Which AI Platforms Should You Measure?

By CassaInnova · Updated 20 September 2026

Track ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google's AI Overviews — the four platforms an SEO consultant already checks manually. Together they cover the largest share of AI-answer traffic likely to reach a client's buyers. Bing Copilot and Meta AI are optional additions, worth including only if a client's audience skews toward them.

Why These Four Platforms, and Not a Longer List?

Each one earns its place for a different reason, not just size.

  • ChatGPT. The largest consumer base by a wide margin, and the platform clients ask about first — usually the "let me look into that" question is literally about ChatGPT.
  • Perplexity. Built around citing sources directly, which makes its answers unusually easy to audit and easy for a client to verify themselves.
  • Gemini. Increasingly folded into Google's own search and Workspace products, giving it reach beyond a standalone chat app.
  • Google AI Overviews. Sits directly inside the search results a client already tracks rankings in, making it the hardest one to ignore.

What About Bing Copilot, Meta AI, and Other Platforms?

  • Bing Copilot is worth adding for a client whose buyers skew toward Microsoft's ecosystem, but it's a smaller addition for most categories.
  • Meta AI matters mainly for consumer brands selling inside Facebook, Instagram, or WhatsApp directly.
  • Neither belongs in every engagement. Adding a platform that returns nothing for the client's category burns audit time without adding a finding.

Does the Platform List Change by Client or Industry?

Yes — the fixed four are a floor, not a ceiling that fits every client identically.

Should the Platform List Ever Shrink Mid-Engagement?

  • Drop a platform only after it's tested, not on assumption. A platform returning nothing for every question in the battery is itself a finding, worth logging rather than quietly skipping.
  • Keep the evidence either way. A client asking "what about Gemini" deserves a logged answer, even if that answer is "not present, checked on this date."
